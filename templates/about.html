About the Animal Rescue & Adoption System

A web-based system that improves how rescue and adoption work is organized in our community.

Objectives

General objective: To improve the organization and management of animal rescue and adoption activities within the community.

Specific objectives:

  • To provide a feature for recording and tracking animal rescue incidents within the community.
  • To generate reports about rescued animals, available animals, and completed adoptions.
  • To maintain animal medical records, including vaccinations, treatments, and health conditions.

System features

Rescue Incident Module

Record animal rescue reports: rescue location (with map pin), date, animal details and condition.

Animal Profile Module

Stores each animal's species, breed, age, medical history and current status.

Medical Records Module

Staff record examinations, vaccinations, treatments, medications and follow-up appointments.

Adoption Management Module

Tracks adoption applications, adopter profiles, application status and completed adoptions.

Dashboard Module

Summaries of rescued animals, animals under care, animals available for adoption and completed adoptions.

Report Generator Module

Reports by date, location, animal type and status, exportable to CSV.

Account Management Module

Administrators create and manage user accounts and access permissions (admin, staff, vet, volunteer).

Also included

Found-pet reports, rehoming requests, volunteer & foster applications, supply wishlist & pledges, request tracking, email notices and a rescue map.

Technical components

Front end

Programming language: Python (web pages built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript templates)

Back end

Database application: MySQL

Budget (if applicable)

Proposed budget: to be filled in by the project team

Human resource - knowledge needed

  • Animal rescue and intake process
  • Animal adoption procedures
  • Animal health and vaccination records

The system has no online payment and no automatic dispatch: a staff member always reviews and responds to each request.
