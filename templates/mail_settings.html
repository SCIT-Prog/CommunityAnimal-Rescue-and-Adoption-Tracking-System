Connect a Gmail account so the system can email applicants when staff approve or decline, and alert you when someone sends a new request.

How to get a Gmail App Password

  1. Open your Google Account, then Security.
  2. Turn on 2-Step Verification (required by Google).
  3. Search for App passwords, create one named “Rescue System”.
  4. Copy the 16 letters Google shows and paste them below. Do not use your normal Gmail password.
